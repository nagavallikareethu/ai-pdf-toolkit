For English Language:
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. What is the capital of France?
A) London
B) Berlin
C) Paris
D) Madrid
Answer: C

2. Which programming language is used for web development?
A) COBOL
B) Fortran
C) JavaScript
D) Assembly
Answer: C

3. What is 2 + 2?
A) 3
B) 4
C) 5
D) 6
Answer: B
